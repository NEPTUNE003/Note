1. 二端口网络
	（1）一端口网络：端口由一对端钮构成，且满足条件：从一个端钮流入的电流等于从另一个端钮流出的电流。
	（2）二端口网络：当一个电路与外部电路通过两个端口连接时（二端口一定有四个端子，四个端子不一定是二端口，还有可能是四端网络）
2. 二端口的方程与参数
	（1）Y形（适$\pi$）
	（2）Z形（适T）
	（3）互易二端口：Y12＝Y21（无受控源才满足）（只有三个独立）
		对称二端口：Y12＝Y21，Y11＝Y22（只有两个独立）
	（4）方法
		1）列方程，提矩阵
		Z：
		$$
		\begin{cases}
		\dot{U}_1 = Z_{11}\dot{I}_1 + Z_{12}\dot{I}_2 \\
		\dot{U}_2 = Z_{21}\dot{I}_1 + Z_{22}\dot{I}_2
		\end{cases}
		$$
		Y：
		$$
		\begin{cases}
		\dot{I}_1 = Y_{11}\dot{U}_1 + Y_{12}\dot{U}_2 \\
		\dot{I}_2 = Y_{21}\dot{U}_1 + Y_{22}\dot{U}_2
		\end{cases}
		$$
		T：
		$$
		\begin{cases}
		\dot{U}_1 = A\dot{U}_2 - B\dot{I}_2 \\
		\dot{I}_1 = C\dot{U}_2 - D\dot{I}_2
		\end{cases}
		$$
		H：
		$$
		\begin{cases}
		\dot{U}_1 = H_{11}\dot{I}_1 + H_{12}\dot{U}_2 \\
		\dot{I}_2 = H_{21}\dot{I}_1 + H_{22}\dot{U}_2
		\end{cases}
		$$
		2）直接列
		Z：
		$$
		\begin{aligned}
		Z_{11} &= \left.\frac{\dot{U}_1}{\dot{I}_1}\right|_{\dot{I}_2=0} & \quad Z_{12} &= \left.\frac{\dot{U}_1}{\dot{I}_2}\right|_{\dot{I}_1=0} \\
		Z_{21} &= \left.\frac{\dot{U}_2}{\dot{I}_1}\right|_{\dot{I}_2=0} & \quad Z_{22} &= \left.\frac{\dot{U}_2}{\dot{I}_2}\right|_{\dot{I}_1=0}
		\end{aligned}
		$$
		Y：
		$$
		\begin{aligned}
		Y_{11} &= \left.\frac{\dot{I}_1}{\dot{U}_1}\right|_{\dot{U}_2=0} & \quad Y_{12} &= \left.\frac{\dot{I}_1}{\dot{U}_2}\right|_{\dot{U}_1=0} \\
		Y_{21} &= \left.\frac{\dot{I}_2}{\dot{U}_1}\right|_{\dot{U}_2=0} & \quad Y_{22} &= \left.\frac{\dot{I}_2}{\dot{U}_2}\right|_{\dot{U}_1=0}
		\end{aligned}
		$$
		T：
		$$
		\begin{aligned}
		A &= \left.\frac{\dot{U}_1}{\dot{U}_2}\right|_{\dot{I}_2=0} & \quad B &= \left.\frac{\dot{U}_1}{-\dot{I}_2}\right|_{\dot{U}_2=0} \\
		C &= \left.\frac{\dot{I}_1}{\dot{U}_2}\right|_{\dot{I}_2=0} & \quad D &= \left.\frac{\dot{I}_1}{-\dot{I}_2}\right|_{\dot{U}_2=0}
		\end{aligned}
		$$
		H：
		$$
		\begin{aligned}
		H_{11} &= \left.\frac{\dot{U}_1}{\dot{I}_1}\right|_{\dot{U}_2=0} & \quad H_{12} &= \left.\frac{\dot{U}_1}{\dot{U}_2}\right|_{\dot{I}_1=0} \\
		H_{21} &= \left.\frac{\dot{I}_2}{\dot{I}_1}\right|_{\dot{U}_2=0} & \quad H_{22} &= \left.\frac{\dot{I}_2}{\dot{U}_2}\right|_{\dot{I}_1=0}
		\end{aligned}
		$$
